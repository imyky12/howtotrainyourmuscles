Biceps · Dumbbell

How to do the incline dumbbell curl

The incline dumbbell curl is a dumbbell exercise for the biceps (long head). It mainly works the biceps (long head), with help from the forearms. Lean back. The stretch is the exercise.

Muscles worked

  • Biceps (long head)main
  • Forearmshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower to a full stretch 2 sec

    Lower the dumbbells slowly until the arms hang straight down.

  2. Arms hang straight pause one second

    Pause a second in the stretch: the elbows are behind the body, which stretches the long head.

  3. Curl without moving the elbows palms up

    Curl up. The elbows stay back; only the forearms move.

  4. Squeeze at shoulder level

    Squeeze with the dumbbells level with the front of the shoulders.

3 common incline dumbbell curl mistakes

1Elbows swing forward

The elbows swing about 35° forward as you curl.

Right away
The stretch that makes the exercise special is gone.
Over time
The same growth as any curl, with a sore front shoulder.
The fix
Elbows stay behind you.

2Shoulders roll off the pad

The shoulders lift 5–8 cm off the bench as you curl.

Right away
The front shoulders hold the weight; the biceps lose tension.
Over time
Irritation at the front of the shoulder.
The fix
Shoulder blades glued to the bench.

3Too heavy, half range

The arms stop about 30° short of straight at the bottom.

Right away
You skip the stretched third.
Over time
Less growth.
The fix
Lighter dumbbells, full stretch.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12 with dumbbells 30–40% lighter than your standing curl. The bench is set to 50° for this one.

Workouts with the incline dumbbell curl

  • Biceps: Peak and stretch 3 × 10–12, rest 90 s
  • Biceps: Cables and isolation 3 × 10–12, rest 90 s
