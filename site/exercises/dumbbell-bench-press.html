How to do the dumbbell bench press

The dumbbell bench press is a dumbbell exercise for the whole chest. It mainly works the chest, with help from the front shoulder and triceps.

Muscles worked

  • Chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping
  • Tricepshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower the dumbbells 2 sec, breathe in

    Lower both dumbbells slowly, elbows about 45° from your body.

  2. Level with the chest a deep stretch

    Stop when the dumbbells are level with your chest. Dumbbells let you go a little deeper than a bar, so the chest stretches more.

  3. Press up and in breathe out

    Press up and slightly in, breathing out.

  4. Arms straight 10 cm apart

    Finish over your shoulders, dumbbells about 10 cm apart. Don’t bang them together.

Setup checklist

  • Kick the dumbbells up from your knees, then lie back.
  • Shoulder blades back, feet flat.
  • Lower until the dumbbells are level with your chest.
  • Press up and in; stop about 10 cm apart.

3 common dumbbell bench press mistakes

1Dumbbells sink too deep

The dumbbells sink well below the chest and the elbows drop under the bench.

Right away
The front of the shoulder is stretched hard under load.
Over time
A common way to strain the shoulder.
The fix
Stop when the dumbbells are level with your chest.

2Elbows straight out (90°)

Arms make a T. The bar comes down high on the chest.

Right away
The front of your shoulders takes the weight; your chest works less.
Over time
Often leads to pain at the front of the shoulder. With heavy weight, a higher risk of a shoulder strain or a torn chest muscle.
The fix
Elbows about 45° from your body, like an arrow.

3Dumbbells clank together

The dumbbells bang together at the top of every rep.

Right away
The chest relaxes at the top and the weights can bounce out of control.
Over time
Less tension on the chest each rep, and jolts to the wrists and elbows.
The fix
Stop about 10 cm apart, right over your shoulders.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–12 with dumbbells you can get into position safely.

Workouts with the dumbbell bench press

  • Chest: Dumbbells 3 × 8–12, rest 2 min
