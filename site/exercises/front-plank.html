How to do the front plank

The front plank is a bodyweight exercise for the abs (anti-arch). It mainly works the abs and obliques, with help from the glutes. Lie down. Don’t relax. Welcome to the plank.

Muscles worked

  • Absmain
  • Obliquesmain

Step by step

  1. Elbows under the shoulders forearms down

    Elbows directly under the shoulders, forearms flat.

  2. Feet back body straight

    Feet back one at a time, toes tucked: one straight line from head to heels.

  3. Squeeze glutes tuck the tailbone

    Squeeze the glutes and tuck the tailbone a little, ribs down.

  4. Breathe and hold 20–45 s

    Breathe normally and hold 20–45 seconds. Brace the whole belly, don’t just suck it in.

3 common front plank mistakes

1Hips sag

The hips drop about 10 cm below the line and the lower back arches.

Right away
The abs let go and the lower back hangs.
Over time
A sore lower back instead of ab work.
The fix
Squeeze glutes, tuck the tailbone.

2Hips piked

The hips sit 10–15 cm above the line, like a tent.

Right away
The abs are off the hook; the shoulders hold you.
Over time
No progress.
The fix
Lower the hips, long body.

3Head drops

The head drops toward the floor and the shoulders sink toward the ears.

Right away
Neck strain; the shoulder-blade muscles switch off.
Over time
Neck ache.
The fix
Eyes on the hands, push the floor away.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 holds of 20–30 s (or from the knees). Add 5 s a week; past 45–60 s, try the elbows 10–15 cm further forward instead.

Workouts with the front plank

  • Core: All three jobs 1 × 30–45 s, rest 45 s
  • Core: Mat only, back friendly 3 × 20–40 s, rest 45 s
