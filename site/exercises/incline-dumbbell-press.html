How to do the incline dumbbell press

The incline dumbbell press is a dumbbell exercise for the upper chest. It mainly works the upper chest, with help from the front shoulder and triceps.

Muscles worked

  • Upper chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping
  • Tricepshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower the dumbbells 2 sec, breathe in

    Bench at 30°. Lower both dumbbells slowly toward the top of your chest.

  2. Level with the upper chest stretch

    Stop with the dumbbells level with your upper chest.

  3. Press up and in breathe out

    Press up and slightly in, breathing out.

  4. Arms straight over the collarbones

    Finish above your collarbones, dumbbells about 10 cm apart.

Setup checklist

  • Bench at 30°.
  • Shoulder blades back, feet flat, bottom on the seat.
  • Lower until the dumbbells are level with your upper chest.
  • Press up and in above your collarbones.

2 common incline dumbbell press mistakes

1Bench too steep (60°)

The bench is raised to about 60°, so it turns into a shoulder press.

Right away
The front shoulders take over from the upper chest.
Over time
Less upper-chest growth, more shoulder strain.
The fix
Set the bench to 30–45°.

2Dumbbells sink too deep

The dumbbells sink far below the chest at the bottom.

Right away
The front of the shoulder is stretched hard under load.
Over time
A common way to strain the shoulder.
The fix
Stop when the dumbbells are level with your upper chest.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–12.

Workouts with the incline dumbbell press

  • Chest: Dumbbells 3 × 8–12, rest 2 min
