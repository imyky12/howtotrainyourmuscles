How to do the cable lateral raise

The cable lateral raise is a cable exercise for the side shoulders. It mainly works the side shoulder, with help from the upper traps. Tension at the bottom too. The cable never rests.

Step by step

  1. Lower across the body 3 sec

    Lower the handle slowly back across the front of the body.

  2. Handle at the far hip stretch

    Hand by the opposite hip: the cable keeps the side shoulder loaded even here.

  3. Sweep out to shoulder height elbow leads

    Sweep the arm out and slightly forward to shoulder height.

  4. Hold one second shoulder down

    Hold a second. We show the right arm; then turn round for the left. It grows the shoulder the same as dumbbells; the cable just keeps tension at the bottom.

3 common cable lateral raise mistakes

1Leaning into the tower

The torso tilts 20–30° toward the tower and the hips swing out.

Right away
The body does the lifting.
Over time
No extra shoulder, and strain on the lower back.
The fix
Hips still; only the arm moves.

2Bending the elbow

The elbow bends from about 165° to 110° as the arm rises.

Right away
A shorter lever means less shoulder work.
Over time
The biceps grow; the shoulders don’t.
The fix
The elbow angle never changes.

3Shrugging

The working shoulder rises 4–6 cm toward the ear.

Right away
The upper traps take over.
Over time
Tight traps, flat shoulders.
The fix
Shoulder down, reach wide.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 12–15 each arm on the lightest plates.

Workouts with the cable lateral raise

  • Shoulders: No barbell 3 × 12–15 each arm, rest 60–90 s
