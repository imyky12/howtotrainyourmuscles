How to do the hanging knee raise

The hanging knee raise is a bodyweight exercise for the abs (lower bias). It mainly works the abs and obliques, with help from the hip flexors. Hang around and bring your knees to the party.

Muscles worked

  • Absmain
  • Obliquesmain
  • Hip flexorshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 2 sec, no swing

    Lower the legs slowly, about 2 seconds, without swinging.

  2. Hang tall shoulders down

    Hang tall with the shoulders pushed down.

  3. Knees up to the chest curl the pelvis

    Draw the knees up and curl the pelvis so the lower back rounds off the pad.

  4. Squeeze one second

    Squeeze for a second. You can bias the lower part of the abs a little this way; it is still one muscle.

3 common hanging knee raise mistakes

1Swinging

The body swings 15–20 cm on every rep and the reps use momentum.

Right away
Momentum, not the abs.
Over time
No progress; your grip tires first.
The fix
Pause at the bottom, no swing.

2Knees only, no curl

The knees come up but the pelvis never curls.

Right away
The hip flexors do all the work.
Over time
Tight hip flexors, undertrained abs.
The fix
Curl the hips up, not just the knees.

3Shrugged shoulders

The shoulders ride up toward the ears.

Right away
The neck and traps hold you up.
Over time
Neck strain.
The fix
Push the shoulders down.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–12 in the captain’s chair. If your lower back hurts, go back to the dead bug.

Workouts with the hanging knee raise

  • Core: All three jobs 3 × 8–12, rest 90 s
  • Core: Mat only, back friendly 1 × 10–12, rest —
