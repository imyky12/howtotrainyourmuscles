How to do the pallof press

The pallof press is a cable exercise for the obliques (anti-twist). It mainly works the obliques and abs. The cable wants you to twist. Politely refuse.

Muscles worked

  • Obliquesmain

Step by step

  1. Bring it back to the chest

    Bring the handle back to the middle of your chest.

  2. Handle at the chest cable taut

    Handle at the breastbone, side-on to the tower, cable taut.

  3. Press straight out don’t twist

    Press straight out to arm’s length. The cable tries to twist you; don’t let it.

  4. Hold two seconds

    Hold for two seconds, belly button facing forward. Then the other side.

3 common pallof press mistakes

1Trunk twists

The shoulders rotate 20–30° toward the tower at full reach.

Right away
The cable wins and the obliques give up.
Over time
The drill trains nothing.
The fix
Belly button faces the front.

2Leaning away

The torso bends about 15° away from the tower to counterweight the cable.

Right away
Body weight does the anti-twist work.
Over time
No oblique work.
The fix
Stand tall, stay centred.

3Hands drift

The handle ends about 10 cm off the middle of the chest, toward the tower.

Right away
A shorter lever, so it gets easier.
Over time
Less and less load over time.
The fix
Press straight from your breastbone.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–10 per side with the lightest plate. Only a small pilot study exists; this is mostly coaching practice.

Workouts with the pallof press

  • Core: All three jobs 2 × 8–10 each side, rest 60 s
