How to do the push-up

The push-up is a bodyweight exercise for the whole chest. It mainly works the chest, with help from the front shoulder and triceps.

Muscles worked

  • Chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping
  • Tricepshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower your chest 2 sec, breathe in

    Body in one straight line, hands just wider than your shoulders. Lower slowly, elbows about 45° from your body.

  2. Chest near the floor about 5 cm

    Stop with your chest about 5 cm from the floor.

  3. Push the floor away breathe out

    Push the floor away, breathing out. Keep your hips in line.

  4. Arms straight shoulder blades spread

    Arms straight at the top and spread your shoulder blades.

Setup checklist

  • Hands just wider than your shoulders.
  • One straight line from head to heels: squeeze glutes and abs.
  • Lower until your chest is about 5 cm from the floor, elbows 45°.
  • Push back up to straight arms.

4 common push-up mistakes

1Hips sag

The hips drop toward the floor, like a banana.

Right away
Your lower back bends and takes the strain.
Over time
Lower-back pain.
The fix
Squeeze your glutes and abs: one straight line from head to heels.

2Hips piked up

The hips stick up in the air.

Right away
Less of your body weight is on your arms, so the chest works less.
Over time
Slower progress for the same effort.
The fix
Lower the hips into one straight line.

3Elbows straight out (90°)

The elbows point straight out to the sides, like a T.

Right away
The front of your shoulders takes the weight; your chest works less.
Over time
Often leads to pain at the front of the shoulder. With heavy weight, a higher risk of a shoulder strain or a torn chest muscle.
The fix
Keep the elbows about 45° from your body, like an arrow.

4Half push-up

The chest stops well above the floor; the elbows bend only to about 90°.

Right away
The chest skips the bottom of the range, where it is stretched and works hardest.
Over time
Slower progress, and full push-ups never get easier.
The fix
Lower until your chest is about 5 cm from the floor.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of as many clean reps as you can, minus two. Too hard? Put your hands on a bench.
