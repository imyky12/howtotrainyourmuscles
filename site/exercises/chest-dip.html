How to do the chest dip

The chest dip is a bodyweight exercise for the lower chest. It mainly works the lower chest, with help from the front shoulder and triceps.

Muscles worked

  • Lower chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping
  • Tricepshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower yourself 2 sec, lean forward

    Lean your body forward a little, about 20–30°. Bend the elbows and lower yourself slowly.

  2. Upper arms level with the floor

    Stop when your upper arms are about level with the floor.

  3. Press back up breathe out

    Press back up, breathing out, without swinging.

  4. Arms straight shoulders down

    Arms straight, shoulders pushed down away from your ears.

Setup checklist

  • Arms straight, shoulders down, feet crossed behind you.
  • Lean forward 20–30° for the chest version.
  • Lower until the upper arms are level with the floor.
  • Press back up without swinging.

2 common chest dip mistakes

1Going too deep

The shoulders drop far below the elbows at the bottom.

Right away
The front of the shoulder is stretched hard under your whole body weight.
Over time
One of the most common causes of shoulder pain from dips.
The fix
Stop when your upper arms are about level with the floor.

2Shoulders up to the ears

The shoulders shrug up toward the ears.

Right away
The neck and the top of the shoulders take the strain.
Over time
Neck and shoulder pain.
The fix
Push your shoulders down, away from your ears, before every rep.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 6–10 on an assisted dip machine, or slow lowering-only reps.

Workouts with the chest dip

  • Chest: Classic barbell 2 × 8–12, rest 2 min
  • Chest: Dumbbells 2 × 6–10, rest 2 min
  • Chest: No equipment 3 × 6–10, rest 2 min
