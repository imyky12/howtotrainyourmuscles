How to do the straight-arm pulldown

The straight-arm pulldown is a cable exercise for the lats. It mainly works the lats, with help from the triceps (long head). Straight arms, soft elbows, lats do the sweeping.

Muscles worked

  • Latsmain
  • Triceps (long head)helping

Step by step

  1. Rise slowly 3 sec

    Let the bar rise slowly in an arc, about 3 seconds. The elbows keep the same soft bend.

  2. Stretch hands at head height

    Hands at head height, lats stretched, torso leaning forward about 20°.

  3. Sweep to the thighs arms straight

    Sweep the bar down in an arc to the front of your thighs. The arms stay straight.

  4. Squeeze bar at the thighs

    Bar on the thighs. Squeeze the lats for a moment.

3 common straight-arm pulldown mistakes

1Bending the elbows

The elbows bend from 165° to about 90° during the pull.

Right away
The triceps push the bar and the lats stop isolating.
Over time
No lat benefit from the exercise.
The fix
Soft elbows, keep them still.

2Standing upright

Standing bolt upright, so the bar meets the thighs with the arms still in front.

Right away
The last part of the lat squeeze is cut off.
Over time
A weaker finish and no extra growth.
The fix
Lean forward; bar to the thighs.

3Body bobbing

The torso dips 15–20° on every pull to help the weight down.

Right away
The stretch at the top gets yanked.
Over time
Irritation at the front of the shoulder from bouncing out of the stretch.
The fix
Half the weight, double the control.

Sets and reps for beginners

2–3 sets of 12–15, light: you should feel the lats, not the arms.

Workouts with the straight-arm pulldown

  • Back: Full back 2 × 12–15, rest 60 s
  • Back: No barbell 2 × 12–15, rest 60 s
