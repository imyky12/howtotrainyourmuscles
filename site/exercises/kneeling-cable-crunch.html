How to do the kneeling cable crunch

The kneeling cable crunch is a cable exercise for the abs. It mainly works the abs, with help from the obliques. Kneel, bow to the cable, repeat fifteen times.

Step by step

  1. Return slowly abs stretched

    Come back up slowly until the abs are stretched; the stack stays off the pile.

  2. Rope by the head hips still

    Rope against the head, hips fixed about 30° short of straight.

  3. Crunch ribs to knees curl the spine

    Curl the ribs toward the pelvis. The spine bends; the hips don’t move.

  4. Squeeze elbows to thighs

    Elbows reach the middle of the thighs. Squeeze for a second.

3 common kneeling cable crunch mistakes

1Hinging at the hips

The torso folds at the hips with a straight back instead of curling.

Right away
The hip flexors and lats move the weight.
Over time
The abs grow nothing and the hip flexors tighten.
The fix
Curl the spine; the hips don’t move.

2Pulling with the arms

The hands drift about 15 cm away from the head and the arms pull the rope.

Right away
It becomes a lat pulldown.
Over time
Nothing for the abs.
The fix
Rope glued to your head.

3Sitting back

The bottom drops back toward the heels at the bottom of each rep.

Right away
Momentum comes from the hips.
Over time
No ab stimulus.
The fix
Stay tall on the knees.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–15 with a light stack. Kneel on a mat. No study has measured this one yet; it is coaching practice.
