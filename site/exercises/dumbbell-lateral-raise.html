How to do the dumbbell lateral raise

The dumbbell lateral raise is a dumbbell exercise for the side shoulders. It mainly works the side shoulders, with help from the rear shoulders and upper traps. Elbows lead, body still. Light weight, big shoulders.

Muscles worked

  • Side shouldersmain
  • Rear shouldershelping
  • Upper trapshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 2 sec

    Lower the dumbbells slowly, about 2 seconds.

  2. Arms just off the sides keep tension

    Stop just before they touch your legs, so the shoulders stay loaded.

  3. Raise to shoulder height elbows lead

    Raise the arms out and slightly forward until the elbows are level with the shoulders. Elbows lead, soft bend.

  4. Hold one second shoulders down

    Hold a second with the shoulders down, then lower.

3 common dumbbell lateral raise mistakes

1Swinging

The torso rocks forward and back on every rep and the dumbbells fly above the shoulders.

Right away
Momentum, not the side shoulders, lifts the weight.
Over time
More weight on the rack, same shoulders.
The fix
Lighter weight, body still.

2Shrugging

The shoulders rise 4–6 cm toward the ears as the arms go up.

Right away
The upper traps steal the work.
Over time
Tight traps, flat shoulders.
The fix
Shoulders down, reach wide.

3Pouring the jug

The hands rise above the elbows with the thumbs turned down, like pouring a jug.

Right away
It shifts the load to the rear shoulders and traps.
Over time
Thumbs-down raises at the top are the position most shoulder advice warns against.
The fix
Elbows lead, thumbs level.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 12–15 with 2–5 kg. Stop at shoulder height; no swinging.

Workouts with the dumbbell lateral raise

  • Shoulders: Barbell and raises 3 × 12–15, rest 60–90 s
