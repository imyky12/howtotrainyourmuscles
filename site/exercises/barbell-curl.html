Biceps · Barbell

How to do the barbell curl

The barbell curl is a barbell exercise for the biceps. It mainly works the biceps, with help from the forearms. The oldest exercise in the mirror. Still works.

Muscles worked

  • Bicepsmain
  • Forearmshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 2 sec

    Lower the bar slowly, about 2 seconds, until the arms are almost straight.

  2. Arms almost straight bar at the thighs

    Bar at the thighs, elbows just in front of the ribs.

  3. Curl to the chest elbows still

    Curl the bar up to the upper chest. Only the forearms move.

  4. Squeeze one second

    Squeeze for a second at the top, then lower.

3 common barbell curl mistakes

1Body swing

The torso leans back 15–25° and the hips push forward as the bar goes up.

Right away
The lower back and hips lift the bar; the biceps relax.
Over time
The weight climbs faster than your arms earn it; lower-back strain.
The fix
Elbows still, chest tall.

2Elbows drift forward

The elbows travel forward and up so the upper arms reach about 35° at the top.

Right away
The front shoulders take over at the top.
Over time
Tired front shoulders and stalled curl progress.
The fix
Pin the elbows to your sides.

3Half reps

The elbows stay bent about 45° at the bottom.

Right away
You skip the stretched range that drives growth.
Over time
Less growth per set.
The fix
Straighten the arms every rep.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–12 with the empty bar or a 10 kg fixed bar. If your wrists ache, use the EZ bar.

Workouts with the barbell curl

  • Biceps: Peak and stretch 3 × 8–10, rest 90 s
