How to do the dumbbell kickback

The dumbbell kickback is a dumbbell exercise for the triceps (long head). It mainly works the triceps, with help from the rear shoulder. Light weight, straight arm, one honest second.

Muscles worked

  • Tricepsmain
  • Rear shoulderhelping

Step by step

  1. Lower the forearm 1 sec

    Lower only the forearm until it hangs straight down.

  2. Forearm hangs upper arm level

    Upper arm level with your back, elbow by the ribs.

  3. Kick back straight arm

    Straighten the arm back until it is one straight line.

  4. Squeeze one second

    Squeeze for a second: that squeeze is the whole exercise. We show the right arm; then swap.

3 common dumbbell kickback mistakes

1Upper arm drops

The elbow sags so the upper arm points down at the floor.

Right away
Gravity no longer loads the lockout, so the rep gets easy.
Over time
No stimulus.
The fix
Elbow higher than your back.

2Swinging the upper arm

The upper arm swings about 35° every rep, like a row.

Right away
The rear shoulder and lats do a half row.
Over time
Nothing for the triceps.
The fix
Only the forearm moves.

3Half lockout

The elbow stops about 30° short of straight.

Right away
The top squeeze, the whole point, never happens.
Over time
Wasted sets.
The fix
Straight arm, one second.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 12–15 per arm with 2–6 kg. It is a light exercise by design.

Workouts with the dumbbell kickback

  • Triceps: Free weights only 3 × 12–15 each arm, rest 60 s
