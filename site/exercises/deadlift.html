How to do the deadlift

The deadlift is a barbell exercise for the lower back. It mainly works the lower back, glutes and hamstrings. Chest and hips rise together, like good neighbours.

Muscles worked

  • Lower backmain
  • Glutesmain
  • Hamstringsmain

Step by step

  1. Hinge back, lower 2 sec

    Push the hips back first and slide the bar down the thighs; bend the knees once it passes them.

  2. Plates touch reset the brace

    Plates touch the floor. Bar over the middle of your feet, back flat, take a breath.

  3. Push the floor away chest and hips together

    Push the floor away. Chest and hips rise together, the bar slides up the legs.

  4. Stand tall hips through

    Hips forward, stand tall. No leaning back.

3 common deadlift mistakes

1Hips shoot up

The hips rise first and the torso tips forward before the bar leaves the floor.

Right away
It becomes a stiff-leg lift with a longer lever on the back.
Over time
The lower back limits every set before the legs do.
The fix
Chest and hips rise together.

2Rounded back

The lower back rounds by 20–30° as the bar leaves the floor.

Right away
More shear load on the lumbar discs.
Over time
Repeated heavy rounding is where most lifters’ back trouble starts.
The fix
Big breath, chest up, flat back.

3Bar drifts forward

The bar drifts 10–15 cm in front of the feet as it passes the knees.

Right away
A longer lever for the hips and back.
Over time
Slower progress and a sore back.
The fix
Drag the bar up the legs.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 5 with the bar and light plates. Learn the hinge with 10–20 kg first.

Workouts with the deadlift

  • Back: Full back 3 × 5, rest 3 min
