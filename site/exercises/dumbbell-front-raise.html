How to do the dumbbell front raise

The dumbbell front raise is a dumbbell exercise for the front shoulders. It mainly works the front shoulders, with help from the upper chest. Raise to the shoulder, stop. Higher is just showing off.

Muscles worked

  • Front shouldersmain
  • Upper chesthelping

Step by step

  1. Lower to the thighs 2 sec

    Lower the dumbbells slowly to the front of the thighs.

  2. Dumbbells on the thighs reset

    Dumbbells against the thighs, palms facing you.

  3. Raise to shoulder height arms long

    Raise straight in front to shoulder height, elbows softly bent.

  4. Hold one second shoulders down

    Hold for a second, then lower. Presses already work the front shoulder hard, so this one is optional.

3 common dumbbell front raise mistakes

1Leaning back

The torso leans back about 15° on each raise to swing the dumbbells up.

Right away
Momentum lifts the weight.
Over time
The lower back takes the strain.
The fix
Body still, arms only.

2Above the head

The arms keep going to about 130°, above the head.

Right away
The traps and serratus do the top part.
Over time
Wasted range, nothing dangerous.
The fix
Stop at shoulder height.

3Shrugging

The shoulders rise toward the ears as the arms go up.

Right away
The upper traps take over.
Over time
Tight traps.
The fix
Shoulders down.

Sets and reps for beginners

2 sets of 12–15 with 2–5 kg. Skip it if the front of the shoulder is sore after pressing.

Workouts with the dumbbell front raise

  • Shoulders: Barbell and raises 2 × 12–15 (optional), rest 60 s
