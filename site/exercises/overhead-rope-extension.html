How to do the overhead rope extension

The overhead rope extension is a cable exercise for the triceps (long head). It mainly works the triceps (long head). Arms up. The long head finally stretches.

Muscles worked

  • Triceps (long head)main

Step by step

  1. Lower into the stretch 3 sec

    Lower slowly, about 3 seconds, hands going behind the head.

  2. Hands behind the head pause one second

    Pause for a second in the stretch: this is the point of the exercise.

  3. Extend overhead elbows by the ears

    Straighten the arms overhead. Upper arms stay beside the ears.

  4. Arms straight squeeze

    Arms straight above and slightly in front of the head.

3 common overhead rope extension mistakes

1Elbows flare wide

The elbows drift to about 60 cm apart and point sideways.

Right away
The shoulders and traps share the load.
Over time
Shoulder irritation and less stretch on the long head.
The fix
Elbows narrow, pointing forward.

2Lower-back arch

The lower back arches 20° or more as the weight goes up.

Right away
The back braces for the arms.
Over time
Lower-back strain.
The fix
Ribs down, squeeze the glutes.

3Upper arms drop

The upper arms fall to about 120°, so the hands move in front of the head.

Right away
The long head loses its stretch.
Over time
No overhead benefit.
The fix
Biceps touch the ears.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–15, light (5–15 kg). Limited overhead mobility? Use a single dumbbell on a 60° bench.

Workouts with the overhead rope extension

  • Triceps: Heavy press, then the long head 3 × 10–15, rest 90 s
