How to do the barbell bent-over row

The barbell bent-over row is a barbell exercise for the middle back. It mainly works the middle back and lats, with help from the lower back. Freeze the torso. Only the elbows get to move.

Muscles worked

  • Middle backmain

Step by step

  1. Lower with control 2 sec

    Lower the bar slowly until the arms hang straight. The hinge angle does not change.

  2. Arms straight under the chest

    Arms straight, the bar hangs below your chest. Back flat, knees soft.

  3. Row to the ribs elbows back

    Pull the bar to your lower ribs, elbows travelling back past your body.

  4. Squeeze shoulder blades together

    Bar at the ribs, shoulder blades squeezed together. Then lower.

3 common barbell bent-over row mistakes

1Torso heaving

The torso swings up from about 45° to 20° lean on every pull.

Right away
The lower back and hips lift the bar, not the lats.
Over time
You keep adding weight your back muscles never actually move.
The fix
Freeze the torso, move only the arms.

2Rounded back

The lower back rounds by 20–30°, hips tucked under.

Right away
The spine bends under load and the discs take more shear.
Over time
A stiff, sore lower back after rowing days.
The fix
Chest up, hips back, back flat.

3Elbows flared

The elbows flare out to about 90° and the bar comes up to the chest.

Right away
It turns into a rear-shoulder row; the lats do less.
Over time
The front of the shoulder gets irritated under heavy loads.
The fix
Elbows to the hips.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–10, starting with the empty 20 kg bar. Stop the set when the hinge starts to drift.

Workouts with the barbell bent-over row
