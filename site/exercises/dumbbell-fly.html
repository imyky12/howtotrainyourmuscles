How to do the dumbbell fly

The dumbbell fly is a dumbbell exercise for the chest stretch. It mainly works the chest, with help from the front shoulder.

Muscles worked

  • Chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping

Step by step

  1. Open the arms slowly, 2–3 sec

    Arms above your chest, palms facing each other, a soft bend in the elbows. Open the arms slowly in a wide arc, about 3 seconds.

  2. Feel the stretch arms wide

    Stop when your upper arms are level with your body. Feel the stretch across your chest: this stretched position is what the fly is for.

  3. Hug it back same arc

    Bring the dumbbells back up along the same arc, as if hugging a big tree. The elbow bend never changes.

  4. Squeeze hands together

    Hands meet over your chest without touching. Keep the chest tight.

Setup checklist

  • Lie flat, dumbbells above your chest, palms facing each other.
  • Bend the elbows slightly and keep that bend the whole time.
  • Open the arms until the upper arms are level with your body.
  • Sweep back up along the same arc; don’t press.

3 common dumbbell fly mistakes

1Turns into a press

The elbows bend and straighten, so the fly turns into a dumbbell press.

Right away
The triceps take part of the work and the chest loses its stretch.
Over time
You lift more but train the chest less in the stretched position, which is the point of the fly.
The fix
Lock a soft bend in the elbows and keep it the whole rep.

2Arms go too deep

The arms drop far below the body at the bottom.

Right away
The shoulder joint is pulled to its limit while holding weight.
Over time
A common cause of shoulder strain with flies.
The fix
Stop when your upper arms are level with your body.

3Arms locked straight

The elbows are locked completely straight.

Right away
The long, straight arm puts the strain on the elbows.
Over time
Elbow pain over time.
The fix
Keep a soft bend of about 15–20° in the elbows.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–15 with a weight you could lift 20 times.

Workouts with the dumbbell fly

  • Chest: Dumbbells 2 × 12–15, rest 1–1.5 min
