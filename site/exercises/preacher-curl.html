Biceps · EZ bar

How to do the preacher curl

The preacher curl is an ez bar exercise for the biceps (short head). It mainly works the biceps (short head), with help from the forearms. Confess your half reps. The pad knows.

Muscles worked

  • Biceps (short head)main
  • Forearmshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 2–3 sec

    Lower the bar slowly, 2–3 seconds: this is the hard part.

  2. Almost straight soft elbows

    Arms almost straight at the bottom, never a hard lock.

  3. Curl up elbows on the pad

    Curl up with the elbows pressed into the pad.

  4. Hold one second

    Hold for a second about 10–15 cm short of your face.

3 common preacher curl mistakes

1Elbows lift off the pad

The elbows rise 10–15 cm off the pad at the top and the shoulders shrug.

Right away
The pad no longer fixes the shoulder; the front shoulders and traps join in.
Over time
A lighter load on the biceps and slower progress.
The fix
Elbows welded to the pad.

2Bouncing at the bottom

The bar drops fast into straight arms and is jerked back up.

Right away
The tendon takes the jolt.
Over time
Biceps-tendon strain: the preacher curl’s known risk.
The fix
Lower like it’s fragile.

3Bottom cut short

The bar stops with the elbows still bent about 45°.

Right away
It skips the part of the preacher where the biceps work hardest.
Over time
Less growth near the elbow.
The fix
All the way down, soft lock.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12 with the EZ bar plus 2.5–5 kg a side. Never drop into a locked elbow at the bottom.

Workouts with the preacher curl

  • Biceps: Peak and stretch 3 × 10–12, rest 75 s
