How to do the seated calf raise

The seated calf raise is a machine exercise for the calves (soleus). It mainly works the soleus, with help from the calves (gastrocnemius). Sit down, calves. This one’s for the soleus.

Muscles worked

  • Soleusmain
  • Calves (gastrocnemius)helping

Step by step

  1. Lower the heels 2 sec

    Lower the heels slowly below the step.

  2. Pause in the stretch one second

    Pause for a second with the heels low.

  3. Rise onto the toes high

    Rise onto the balls of your feet, lifting the pad.

  4. Hold one second

    Hold at the top. Standing calf raises grow the soleus just as well; this is the soleus-biased option, not a must.

3 common seated calf raise mistakes

1Bouncing

The heels bounce off the bottom with no pause.

Right away
Tendon rebound does the work.
Over time
No growth.
The fix
Slow at the bottom.

2Half range

The heels stay level with the step.

Right away
No stretch.
Over time
About half the growth.
The fix
Heels all the way down.

3Pad on the kneecap

The pad sits on the kneecaps instead of the thighs.

Right away
It hurts.
Over time
Bruised kneecaps, and people quit the exercise.
The fix
Pad on the thighs, not the knees.

See each mistake next to the right form on the 3D body: open the seated calf raise in 3D.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 12–20. Release and re-engage the stop with the heels up.

Workouts with the seated calf raise
