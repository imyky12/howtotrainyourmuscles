How to do the seated dumbbell press

The seated dumbbell press is a dumbbell exercise for the front shoulders. It mainly works the front shoulders and side shoulders, with help from the triceps. Ears to lockout, every rep. Half reps, half shoulders.

Muscles worked

Step by step

  1. Lower to the ears 2 sec

    Lower both dumbbells slowly to ear height.

  2. Dumbbells at ear height elbows a bit forward

    Dumbbells level with the ears, elbows about 90° and slightly in front of the body.

  3. Press up and in breathe out

    Press up and slightly in, breathing out.

  4. Lock out over the shoulders

    Arms straight, dumbbells about 10 cm apart over the shoulders.

3 common seated dumbbell press mistakes

1Half reps

The dumbbells stop at forehead height, so the elbows never bend past about 120°.

Right away
The stretched part of the press is skipped.
Over time
Less growth from the same sets.
The fix
Dumbbells to the ears.

2Elbows in a T, too deep

The elbows are pinned straight out to the sides and the dumbbells sink below the shoulders.

Right away
More strain on the front of the shoulder and a weaker press.
Over time
Front-shoulder irritation under heavy loads (coaching consensus).
The fix
Elbows slightly forward.

3Arching off the pad

The lower back leaves the pad and the ribs flare.

Right away
It becomes an incline press.
Over time
A sore lower back.
The fix
Ribs down, back on the pad.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–12 with dumbbells you can kick up on your own.

Workouts with the seated dumbbell press

  • Shoulders: No barbell 3 × 8–12, rest 2 min
