How to do the reverse pec deck

The reverse pec deck is a machine exercise for the rear shoulders. It mainly works the rear shoulders, with help from the middle back. Sweep, don’t row. The rear delt is shy, not absent.

Muscles worked

  • Rear shouldersmain
  • Middle backhelping

Step by step

  1. Return slowly 2 sec

    Bring the handles back together slowly.

  2. Hands together don’t let the stack land

    Hands meet in front at shoulder height. The stack stays just off the pile.

  3. Sweep the arms back long arms

    Sweep the arms out and back in a wide arc, elbows softly bent.

  4. Hold one second hands at shoulder height

    Arms just behind the body, hands level with the shoulders. Hold a second.

3 common reverse pec deck mistakes

1Turning it into a row

The elbows bend about 70° and the shoulders rise, so it becomes a row.

Right away
The traps and middle back row the weight; the rear shoulders are bypassed.
Over time
Little rear-shoulder growth.
The fix
Long arms. Sweep, don’t row.

2Hands too high

The hands finish 10–15 cm above the shoulders.

Right away
The upper traps take over.
Over time
The rear shoulders stay undertrained.
The fix
Hands level with the shoulders.

3Letting the stack land

The arms rest at the front and the stack lands on every rep.

Right away
Tension is lost every rep.
Over time
Fewer reps that count.
The fix
Stop just before the plates touch.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 12–15, light. Set the handles to the rear position and sit facing the pad.

Workouts with the reverse pec deck

  • Shoulders: Barbell and raises 3 × 12–15, rest 60–90 s
  • Shoulders: No barbell 3 × 12–15, rest 60–90 s
