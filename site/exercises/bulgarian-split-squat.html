How to do the bulgarian split squat

The bulgarian split squat is a dumbbell exercise for the quads & glutes. It mainly works the quads and glutes, with help from the side glute (balance). The exercise everyone loves to hate.

Muscles worked

  • Quadsmain
  • Glutesmain
  • Side glute (balance)helping

Step by step

  1. Lower straight down 2 sec, torso forward

    Lower straight down with the torso leaning a little forward, about 13°.

  2. Rear knee near the floor front thigh level

    Rear knee a hand’s width above the floor, front thigh about level.

  3. Push through the front foot breathe out

    Push through the whole front foot to stand up.

  4. Stand up hips square

    Back to the top, hips square to the front. We show the left leg; then switch.

3 common bulgarian split squat mistakes

1Front knee caves

The front knee drops 6–8 cm inside the foot at the bottom.

Right away
The side glute gives up and balance goes.
Over time
Knee pain as the sets add up.
The fix
Knee over the second toe.

2Foot too close

The front foot is too close to the bench, so the knee shoots forward and the heel lifts.

Right away
All knee, no hip, and wobbly.
Over time
The glutes miss out.
The fix
Step further out, heel down.

3Hips twist

The pelvis rotates about 20° toward the back leg at the bottom.

Right away
The side glute stops stabilising.
Over time
Uneven load from side to side.
The fix
Hips square, face forward.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–10 per leg, bodyweight first. Add dumbbells once 10 clean reps feel steady.

Workouts with the bulgarian split squat

  • Legs: Full legs 2 × 8–10 each leg, rest 90 s
  • Legs: Glutes and machines 2 × 8–10 each leg, rest 90 s
