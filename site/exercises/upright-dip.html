How to do the upright dip

The upright dip is a bodyweight exercise for the triceps (all heads). It mainly works the triceps, with help from the chest and front shoulder. Stay tall; lean forward and it’s chest day.

Muscles worked

Step by step

  1. Lower yourself 2 sec, upright

    Stay upright, elbows pointing back, and lower yourself slowly.

  2. Upper arms level with the floor

    Stop when the upper arms are about level with the floor.

  3. Press back up elbows back

    Press back up without swinging.

  4. Lock out shoulders down

    Arms straight, shoulders pushed down.

3 common upright dip mistakes

1Elbows flare

The elbows flare to 45–60° and point sideways.

Right away
It turns into a chest dip with extra shoulder load.
Over time
Front-shoulder strain.
The fix
Elbows point behind you.

2Too deep

The shoulders drop well below the elbows at the bottom.

Right away
The front of the shoulder is loaded at the end of its range.
Over time
Shoulder impingement or instability.
The fix
Stop at level.

3Shrugging and swinging

The shoulders rise toward the ears and the legs swing.

Right away
Momentum helps and the neck strains.
Over time
Fewer reps that count, and neck strain.
The fix
Quiet legs, shoulders down.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 5–8, or slow lowering-only reps. Front-shoulder pain? Use the close-grip bench or pushdown instead.

Workouts with the upright dip

  • Triceps: Heavy press, then the long head 2 × 5–8, rest 90 s
  • Triceps: Free weights only 3 × 5–8, rest 2 min
