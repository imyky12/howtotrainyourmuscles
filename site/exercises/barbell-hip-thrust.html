How to do the barbell hip thrust

The barbell hip thrust is a barbell exercise for the glutes. It mainly works the glutes, with help from the hamstrings and quads. Glutes, meet gravity. Gravity, meet the padded bar.

Muscles worked

  • Glutesmain
  • Hamstringshelping
  • Quadshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 1 sec

    Lower the hips toward the floor under control.

  2. Hips near the floor chin tucked

    Hips just off the floor. Upper back on the bench edge, chin tucked.

  3. Drive the hips up squeeze

    Drive the hips up through the heels until the torso is level.

  4. Torso level one second

    Hold a second with the glutes squeezed: torso level, shins vertical.

3 common barbell hip thrust mistakes

1Arched lower back

The hips go past level and the lower back arches, ribs flared, head dropped back.

Right away
The lower back, not the glutes, closes the last part.
Over time
Lower-back ache, and the glutes never fully squeeze.
The fix
Ribs down, squeeze, no arch.

2Feet too far out

The feet are too far out, so the shins lean forward at the top.

Right away
The hamstrings take over.
Over time
Hamstring cramps and less glute work.
The fix
Shins straight up at the top.

3Half thrust

The hips stop well below level, at a hip angle of about 135°.

Right away
The glute’s hardest range, full extension, is skipped.
Over time
Less growth.
The fix
Hips all the way up.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12 with the empty bar (with a pad) or bodyweight first.

Workouts with the barbell hip thrust

  • Legs: Glutes and machines 3 × 8–12, rest 2 min
