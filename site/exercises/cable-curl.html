Biceps · Cable

How to do the cable curl

The cable curl is a cable exercise for the biceps. It mainly works the biceps, with help from the forearms. Tension at the top. The bar never had that.

Muscles worked

  • Bicepsmain
  • Forearmshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 2 sec

    Lower the bar slowly, about 2 seconds.

  2. Arms almost straight stack stays up

    Arms almost straight; the stack stays just off the pile.

  3. Curl to the chest elbows by the ribs

    Curl up to the upper chest, elbows by your ribs.

  4. Squeeze one second

    Squeeze at the top: the cable keeps pulling here, where a barbell goes light.

3 common cable curl mistakes

1Leaning back

The torso leans back 15–25° to pull the stack with the back.

Right away
The lats and lower back take over.
Over time
Lower-back strain; arms untrained.
The fix
Lean in, not back.

2Elbows ride up

The elbows ride up so the upper arms reach about 35° at the top.

Right away
It turns into a front raise with a cable.
Over time
Sore front shoulders.
The fix
Elbows down by the ribs.

3Half reps

The elbows stay bent about 45° at the bottom.

Right away
The stretched part is skipped.
Over time
Less growth per set.
The fix
Straighten the arms every rep.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–15. A good first curl. Step in before you let go of the bar.

Workouts with the cable curl
