Core · Ab wheel

How to do the ab wheel rollout

The ab wheel rollout is an ab wheel exercise for the abs (anti-arch). It mainly works the abs and obliques, with help from the lats. Roll out. Roll back. Question your choices.

Step by step

  1. Roll out 2 sec, hips forward

    Roll the wheel forward, hips moving forward with it.

  2. Hold before the back arches

    Stop with the body nearly straight from knees to hands, before the lower back arches. Hold a second.

  3. Pull back with the abs arms straight

    Pull the wheel back to the knees with the abs. Arms stay straight.

  4. Kneel tall tuck the tailbone

    Back to kneeling, tailbone tucked, wheel under the shoulders.

3 common ab wheel rollout mistakes

1Lower back arches

At full reach the belly drops and the lower back arches.

Right away
The spine takes the load the abs should.
Over time
The classic ab-wheel back strain.
The fix
Ribs down, tuck, stop earlier.

2Hips stay back

The hips never open past about 100°, so the wheel barely rolls out.

Right away
It becomes a shoulder exercise; the abs barely work.
Over time
No progress.
The fix
Hips go forward with the wheel.

3Bending the elbows

The elbows bend to about 110° to drag the wheel back.

Right away
The triceps and lats do the return.
Over time
Elbow and shoulder strain; undertrained abs.
The fix
Arms straight, pull with the belly.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 5–8 to a half rollout; roll further over the weeks. Skip it with a sore lower back.

Workouts with the ab wheel rollout

  • Core: All three jobs 3 × 6–10, rest 90 s
  • Core: Mat only, back friendly 2 × 6–8 (half roll-out), rest 90 s
