How to do the pec deck

The pec deck is a machine exercise for the chest squeeze. It mainly works the chest, with help from the front shoulder.

Watch it in 3D →See the right form and every mistake on a 3D body, with the working muscles lit up Check my form →Your phone camera counts your reps and spots these mistakes

Muscles worked

  • Chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping

Step by step

  1. Open the arms slowly, 2 sec

    Let the arms open slowly until they are just behind your body.

  2. Feel the stretch arms wide

    Feel a stretch across the chest, not pain in the shoulder.

  3. Hug it back same arc

    Bring the handles together in a wide arc in front of your chest.

  4. Squeeze hands together

    Squeeze for a second with the hands together.

Setup checklist

  • Set the seat so the handles are at chest height.
  • Back and head against the pad.
  • Bring the arms together in an arc and squeeze for a second.
  • Open slowly, only until the arms are just behind the body.

2 common pec deck mistakes

1Arms start too far back

The arms open far behind the body at the start of each rep.

Right away
The front of the shoulder is jammed at its limit.
Over time
Shoulder strain, the most common pec-deck injury.
The fix
Only open until the arms are just behind your body.

2Leaning off the pad

The body leans forward, away from the back pad.

Right away
The shoulders and arms take over and the chest works less.
Over time
Less chest work and a rounded-shoulder habit.
The fix
Keep your back and head against the pad.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 12–15.

Workouts with the pec deck

  • Chest: Machines 3 × 12–15, rest 1–1.5 min
