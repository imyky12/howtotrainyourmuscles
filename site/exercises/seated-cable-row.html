Back · Cable

How to do the seated cable row

The seated cable row is a cable exercise for the middle back. It mainly works the middle back and lats, with help from the rear shoulder. Handle to the belly button; the chair does not rock.

Muscles worked

  • Middle backmain
  • Latsmain

Step by step

  1. Reach forward 2 sec, slowly

    Let the handle travel forward slowly until the arms are straight. The torso stays still.

  2. Arms straight shoulder blades forward

    Arms straight, shoulder blades pulled forward by the cable. The middle back is stretched.

  3. Pull to the belly elbows back

    Pull the handle to your belly button, elbows sliding back along the ribs.

  4. Hold one second shoulder blades back

    Handle at the belly, shoulder blades back. Hold a second, then reach forward again.

3 common seated cable row mistakes

1Rocking

The torso rocks 25–30° forward and back on every pull.

Right away
The lower back and momentum move the weight.
Over time
You lift heavier stacks without a bigger back.
The fix
Torso still. Only the arms move.

2Shrugging

The shoulders rise 5–7 cm toward the ears at the end of the pull.

Right away
The upper traps take over from the middle back.
Over time
Tight upper traps, little middle-back growth.
The fix
Shoulders down, elbows back.

3Short pull

The handle stops 10–15 cm in front of the belly.

Right away
The shoulder blades never squeeze together.
Over time
A weaker middle back.
The fix
Handle to the belly button.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12. Lean forward and let the stack rest before you let go of the handle.

Workouts with the seated cable row

  • Back: Full back 2 × 10–12, rest 90 s
  • Back: No barbell 3 × 10–12, rest 90 s
