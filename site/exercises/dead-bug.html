How to do the dead bug

The dead bug is a bodyweight exercise for the deep abs (brace). It mainly works the abs and obliques, with help from the hip flexors. Lie on your back, wave limbs, feel smug.

Muscles worked

  • Absmain
  • Obliquesmain
  • Hip flexorshelping

Step by step

  1. Opposite arm and leg reach slowly

    Lower one arm overhead and the opposite leg toward the floor at the same time, slowly.

  2. Stop before the back lifts heel off the floor

    Stop with the heel a few centimetres off the mat, before the lower back lifts.

  3. Return slowly 2 sec

    Bring them back slowly to the table-top start.

  4. Switch sides back pressed down

    Switch sides. The lower back stays pressed into the mat the whole time.

3 common dead bug mistakes

1Lower back arches

A gap opens under the lower back as the leg goes down.

Right away
The hip flexors win and the abs lose.
Over time
It becomes a hip-flexor drill and the back gets cranky.
The fix
Only as low as the back stays down.

2Too fast

The arm and leg swing out and back in under a second.

Right away
Momentum, no bracing.
Over time
You never learn the brace.
The fix
Slow, like moving through honey.

3Head lifts

The chin tucks hard and the head and shoulders lift off the mat.

Right away
The neck works instead of the abs.
Over time
Neck ache.
The fix
Head heavy on the mat.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 6–8 per side, legs only at first. The best start for anyone with a sore back.
