How to do the back squat

The back squat is a barbell exercise for the quads & glutes. It mainly works the quads and glutes, with help from the adductors. Sit down. Stand up. Repeat until philosophical.

Step by step

  1. Sit down between the heels 2 sec, brace

    Big breath, brace, and sit down between your heels. Knees travel forward and out over the toes; that’s normal.

  2. Hip crease below the knee depth

    Hip crease below the top of the knee. Torso about 40° forward, bar over the middle of the foot.

  3. Drive up breathe out

    Drive up, chest and hips rising together. Breathe out past the hardest part.

  4. Stand tall reset the breath

    Stand tall with straight legs. Breathe and brace for the next rep.

3 common back squat mistakes

1Knees cave in

Both knees drift about 6 cm inward at the bottom, almost touching.

Right away
The adductors take over and the glutes aren’t driving.
Over time
Repeated caving under load is linked with knee pain.
The fix
Push the knees out over the toes.

2Good-morning squat

The hips rise first and the torso tips forward past 60°.

Right away
The load moves from the quads to the lower back.
Over time
The lower back limits your squat; the quads get less work.
The fix
Chest up; knees and hips rise together.

3Half squat

The squat stops with the thighs well above parallel.

Right away
The glutes and adductors miss their hardest range.
Over time
Half squats grew the glutes about a third as much in one study.
The fix
Sit lower: hips below the knees.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 6–10, starting with the empty 20 kg bar. Set the safety arms at the height of the bar at the bottom.

Workouts with the back squat

  • Legs: Full legs 3 × 6–8, rest 3 min
