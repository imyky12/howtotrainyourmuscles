How to do the romanian deadlift

The romanian deadlift is a barbell exercise for the hamstrings. It mainly works the hamstrings and glutes, with help from the lower back. A bow so deep your hamstrings applaud.

Muscles worked

  • Hamstringsmain
  • Glutesmain
  • Lower backhelping

Step by step

  1. Hips back, knees soft 3 sec

    Push the hips back. The bar slides down the thighs; the knees stay softly bent the whole time.

  2. Bar just below the knees hamstrings tight

    Stop when the bar is just below the knees or the back wants to round. The hamstrings are stretched.

  3. Hips forward stand up

    Drive the hips forward to stand up, bar sliding up the legs.

  4. Stand tall bar on the thighs

    Stand tall, bar against the thighs. Breathe, then go again.

3 common romanian deadlift mistakes

1Rounded back

The lower back rounds by about 30° at the bottom and the shoulders slump.

Right away
The spine, not the hamstrings, takes the stretch.
Over time
The classic deadlift-family back strain.
The fix
Chest proud, hinge at the hips.

2Squatting it

The knees bend to about 95° and the hips drop, like a squat.

Right away
It turns into a quad lift.
Over time
The hamstrings never get the stretch that grows them.
The fix
Knees stay soft, not bent.

3Bar drifts forward

The bar floats 8–10 cm in front of the thighs on the way down.

Right away
The lower back works at a long lever.
Over time
The back tires before the hamstrings do.
The fix
Drag the bar down your legs.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–10 with the empty bar. Stop lowering when the back wants to round; never bounce.

Workouts with the romanian deadlift

  • Legs: Full legs 3 × 8–10, rest 2–3 min
