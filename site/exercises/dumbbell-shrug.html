How to do the dumbbell shrug

The dumbbell shrug is a dumbbell exercise for the upper traps. It mainly works the upper traps, with help from the forearms (grip). Straight up, straight down. No rolling, this is not dough.

Muscles worked

  • Upper trapsmain
  • Forearms (grip)helping

Step by step

  1. Lower all the way 2 sec

    Lower the shoulders slowly, about 2 seconds.

  2. Shoulders down arms long

    Let the shoulders drop all the way. Arms straight, like hooks.

  3. Shrug straight up toward the ears

    Shrug straight up toward your ears. No rolling.

  4. Hold one second at the top

    Hold at the top for a second, then lower.

3 common dumbbell shrug mistakes

1Rolling shoulders

The shoulders roll in a circle instead of going straight up.

Right away
The rolling part works against nothing, because gravity pulls straight down.
Over time
Nothing dangerous, just wasted reps; some people get a sore shoulder joint.
The fix
Straight up, straight down.

2Bending the elbows

The elbows bend as you shrug, so the biceps help lift.

Right away
The biceps lift part of the weight.
Over time
Elbow strain with heavy dumbbells.
The fix
Arms are hooks, not levers.

3Head pokes forward

The head moves 5–8 cm forward of the shoulders as you shrug.

Right away
The neck muscles take the strain.
Over time
Neck ache after shrug days.
The fix
Chin level, eyes forward.

Sets and reps for beginners

2–3 sets of 12–15. Slow tempo; the range is small.

Workouts with the dumbbell shrug

  • Back: Full back 2 × 12–15, rest 60 s
  • Back: No barbell 2 × 12–15, rest 60 s
