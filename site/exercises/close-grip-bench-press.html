How to do the close-grip bench press

The close-grip bench press is a barbell exercise for the triceps (all heads). It mainly works the triceps, with help from the chest and front shoulder. Same bench. Narrower hands. Your triceps noticed.

Step by step

  1. Bring it down 2 sec, elbows tucked

    Lower the bar slowly, elbows tucked about 15° from your body.

  2. Touch the lower chest light touch

    Touch lightly on the lower chest, a few centimetres lower than a normal bench press.

  3. Press up elbows brush the ribs

    Press up with the elbows brushing the ribs.

  4. Lock out over the shoulders

    Arms straight over the shoulders.

3 common close-grip bench press mistakes

1Grip too narrow

The hands are under 25 cm apart, wrists bent back, elbows flaring to make room.

Right away
Wrist and elbow strain, and less weight.
Over time
Wrist and elbow tendon pain.
The fix
Shoulder width, not thumbs.

2Elbows flared

The elbows flare to 55–90° and the bar drifts up to the nipple line.

Right away
The chest and front shoulders take the load.
Over time
Shoulder stress; triceps undertrained.
The fix
Elbows brush the ribs.

3Hips lift

The bottom lifts off the bench as you press.

Right away
Shorter range and the lower back arches under load.
Over time
Lower-back strain.
The fix
Bottom down, drive the floor.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 6–10, starting with the empty bar. Hands shoulder-width, not touching.

Workouts with the close-grip bench press

  • Triceps: Heavy press, then the long head 3 × 6–10, rest 2–3 min
