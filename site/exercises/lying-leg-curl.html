How to do the lying leg curl

The lying leg curl is a machine exercise for the hamstrings. It mainly works the hamstrings, with help from the calves. Heels to bum. Hamstrings file a complaint.

Muscles worked

  • Hamstringsmain
  • Calveshelping

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 2 sec

    Lower the roller slowly, about 2 seconds.

  2. Legs nearly straight don’t lock

    Legs almost straight, roller behind the ankles.

  3. Curl heels to the bum hips down

    Curl the heels toward your bum. Hips stay pressed into the pad.

  4. Squeeze one second

    Squeeze at the top for a second.

3 common lying leg curl mistakes

1Hips lift

The hips rise off the pad, the hip angle changes 20–30°, and the back arches to cheat.

Right away
The hip flexors and back do the lift.
Over time
Lower-back ache; hamstrings under-loaded.
The fix
Hips glued to the pad.

2Half curl

The curl stops at about 110° at the knee.

Right away
No peak squeeze.
Over time
Slower growth.
The fix
Heels all the way up.

3Dropping the weight

The roller drops in under half a second and bounces.

Right away
Half the training effect is lost.
Over time
Fewer gains and bruised ankles.
The fix
Two seconds on the way down.

See each mistake next to the right form on the 3D body: open the lying leg curl in 3D.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–15. Roller on the ankles, not the calves. The gym has no seated curl, which grows the hamstrings a bit more.

Workouts with the lying leg curl

  • Legs: Full legs 2 × 10–15, rest 90 s
  • Legs: Glutes and machines 2 × 12–15, rest 90 s
