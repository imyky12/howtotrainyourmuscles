How to do the leg press

The leg press is a machine exercise for the quads & glutes. It mainly works the quads and glutes. A squat, lying down. Still not a nap.

Muscles worked

  • Quadsmain
  • Glutesmain

Step by step

  1. Lower the sled 2 sec

    Lower the sled slowly, knees tracking over the toes.

  2. Knees at 90° back on the pad

    Stop with the knees at about 90°, lower back flat on the pad.

  3. Press up breathe out

    Press through the whole foot, breathing out.

  4. Knees soft at the top don’t lock

    Stop just short of straight knees, then lower again.

3 common leg press mistakes

1Tailbone lifts

The sled goes so deep that the pelvis rolls and the lower back lifts off the pad.

Right away
The lower spine bends under the sled’s load.
Over time
The usual cause of leg-press back strain.
The fix
Stop where your hips stay down.

2Locked knees

The knees snap to fully straight at the top with the sled on them.

Right away
Tension drops off the quads and the joint holds the weight.
Over time
Hyperextension stress on the knee.
The fix
Stop just short of straight.

3Heels lift

The heels lift off the plate at the bottom and the knees drift in.

Right away
The knees and calves take the load.
Over time
Knee irritation.
The fix
Drive through the whole foot.

See each mistake next to the right form on the 3D body: open the leg press in 3D.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12. Learn the safety handles first; never unlock them with the knees locked.

Workouts with the leg press

  • Legs: Glutes and machines 3 × 8–12, rest 2–3 min
