How to do the cable crossover, high to low

The cable crossover, high to low is a cable exercise for the lower chest. It mainly works the lower chest, with help from the front shoulder.

Muscles worked

  • Lower chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping

Step by step

  1. Let the arms open 2 sec

    Lean forward a little. Let the handles pull your arms up and out, slowly.

  2. Stretch arms wide and high

    Arms wide at shoulder height, chest open.

  3. Pull down and in in a wide arc

    Sweep the hands down and together in a wide arc. Elbows keep the same soft bend.

  4. Squeeze hands at the hips

    Hands meet in front of your hips. Squeeze for a second.

Setup checklist

  • Pulleys above head height, one handle in each hand.
  • Staggered stance, lean forward slightly, soft elbows.
  • Sweep the hands down and together in front of your hips.
  • Return slowly with the chest open.

2 common cable crossover, high to low mistakes

1Pressing the handles

The elbows bend and then straighten, pushing the handles like a press.

Right away
The triceps help out and the chest loses its stretch.
Over time
Less chest work from the exercise.
The fix
Keep a soft, fixed bend in the elbows and sweep the arms in an arc.

2Upright, hands to the chest

Standing straight up, with the hands meeting at chest height.

Right away
It turns into a mid-chest fly; the lower chest gets less work.
Over time
The lower chest misses out, and that is the point of this exercise.
The fix
Lean forward a little and bring your hands down to meet in front of your hips.

Sets and reps for beginners

2–3 sets of 12–15. Lower-chest emphasis comes from the direction of the fibres (coaching consensus).

Workouts with the cable crossover, high to low

  • Chest: Classic barbell 2 × 12–15, rest 1–1.5 min
  • Chest: Machines 2 × 12–15, rest 1–1.5 min
