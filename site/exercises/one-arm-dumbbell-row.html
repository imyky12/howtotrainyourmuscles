How to do the one-arm dumbbell row

The one-arm dumbbell row is a dumbbell exercise for the lats. It mainly works the lats and middle back, with help from the biceps. Elbow to the back pocket, shoulders flat as a table.

Step by step

  1. Lower and stretch 2 sec

    Lower the dumbbell slowly until the arm hangs straight.

  2. Arm hangs straight shoulder blade forward

    Let the shoulder blade reach toward the floor. The lat is stretched.

  3. Row to the hip elbow back

    Pull the elbow up and back along your ribs, dumbbell toward the hip. Shoulders stay square.

  4. Squeeze one second

    Dumbbell at the hip, shoulder blade squeezed. Hold a second. We show the right arm; then switch sides.

3 common one-arm dumbbell row mistakes

1Torso twist

The working shoulder rotates up 25–40° to swing the dumbbell up.

Right away
The twist, not the lat, moves the weight.
Over time
One side gets stronger than the other.
The fix
Shoulders flat to the floor.

2Elbow flared

The elbow travels out to the side and the dumbbell ends under the chest.

Right away
The rear shoulder takes over from the lat.
Over time
Slower lat growth.
The fix
Elbow to the back pocket.

3No stretch

The elbow stays bent at about 140° at the bottom.

Right away
No stretched-position work for the lat.
Over time
Less growth per set.
The fix
Let it hang, then row.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12 each side. The bench holds your torso, so keep the back flat.

Workouts with the one-arm dumbbell row

  • Back: No barbell 3 × 10 each side, rest 90 s
