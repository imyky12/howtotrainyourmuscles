How to do the rope pushdown

The rope pushdown is a cable exercise for the triceps (outer heads). It mainly works the triceps. Elbows glued. Only the forearms are allowed to move.

Muscles worked

  • Tricepsmain

Step by step

  1. Return slowly to chest height

    Let the rope rise slowly to chest height.

  2. Elbows at the ribs hands at the chest

    Elbows by the ribs, hands at the lower chest.

  3. Push to the thighs split the rope

    Push the rope down to the thighs, parting the ends at the bottom.

  4. Lock out squeeze

    Arms straight; squeeze for a moment.

3 common rope pushdown mistakes

1Elbows swing

The upper arms swing about 40° forward and back on every rep.

Right away
The lats and shoulders press the stack.
Over time
Progress stalls; elbow irritation.
The fix
Elbows glued, forearms hinge.

2Leaning on the stack

The torso leans forward 30° or more and bobs with every rep.

Right away
Body weight pushes the weight.
Over time
Too heavy, no arm growth.
The fix
Lighter, stand tall.

3Half lockout

The elbows stop about 30° short of straight.

Right away
The last part, the medial head’s, is never loaded.
Over time
Weaker lockouts.
The fix
Straight arms, squeeze.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 12–15: the best first triceps exercise. Step in before you let go of the rope.

Workouts with the rope pushdown

  • Triceps: Heavy press, then the long head 3 × 12–15, rest 60–75 s
  • Triceps: Free weights only 2 × 15, rest 60 s
