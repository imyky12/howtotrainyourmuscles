How to do the cable face pull

The cable face pull is a cable exercise for the rear shoulders. It mainly works the rear shoulders and middle back. Pull the rope to your face, not your ego.

Muscles worked

  • Rear shouldersmain
  • Middle backmain

Step by step

  1. Return slowly 2 sec

    Let the rope travel back slowly until the arms are straight.

  2. Arms straight rope at forehead height

    Arms straight toward the pulley at forehead height.

  3. Pull to the ears elbows high

    Pull the rope apart toward your ears, elbows high and wide.

  4. Rotate up one second

    Finish with the hands beside the ears, rotated up. Hold a second.

3 common cable face pull mistakes

1Elbows dropping

The elbows end down by the ribs, 20–30 cm below the shoulders.

Right away
The lats and middle back row the weight; the rear shoulders are bypassed.
Over time
The rear shoulders stay small.
The fix
Elbows high, pull to the face.

2Leaning back

The torso leans back 20–30° and the hips push forward.

Right away
Body weight pulls the stack.
Over time
Lower-back strain as the pin goes up.
The fix
Lighter pin, body still.

3No rotation

The forearms stay flat and the hands stop in front of the chin.

Right away
The rotator-cuff part of the exercise is skipped.
Over time
Less cuff work from the same sets.
The fix
Show your biceps at the end.

Sets and reps for beginners

2–3 sets of 15–20, light. This is never a heavy exercise.

Workouts with the cable face pull

  • Shoulders: Barbell and raises 2 × 15–20, rest 60 s
  • Shoulders: No barbell 2 × 15–20, rest 60 s
