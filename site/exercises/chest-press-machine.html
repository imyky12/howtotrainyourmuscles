How to do the chest press machine

The chest press machine is a machine exercise for the middle chest. It mainly works the chest, with help from the front shoulder and triceps.

Muscles worked

  • Chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping
  • Tricepshelping

Step by step

  1. Let the handles back 2 sec, breathe in

    Let the handles come back slowly, elbows about 45° below shoulder height.

  2. Handles by the chest stretch

    Stop with the handles just in front of your chest, at nipple height.

  3. Push forward breathe out

    Push forward, breathing out. Keep your back against the pad.

  4. Arms almost straight don’t lock

    Arms almost straight. Don’t lock the elbows hard; start the next rep.

Setup checklist

  • Set the seat so the handles line up with the middle of your chest.
  • Back and head against the pad, feet flat.
  • Push forward until the arms are almost straight.
  • Come back until the handles are just in front of the chest.

2 common chest press machine mistakes

1Seat too low

The seat is too low, so the handles sit at shoulder height.

Right away
Your shoulders shrug up and the front shoulders take over.
Over time
Shoulder strain and less chest work.
The fix
Raise the seat so the handles line up with the middle of your chest.

2Back comes off the pad

At the end of each push the upper back leaves the pad and the shoulders roll forward.

Right away
The shoulders take the load and the chest stops working early.
Over time
Shoulder strain and a rounded-shoulder habit.
The fix
Keep your shoulder blades back against the pad the whole time.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12. A great first chest exercise.

Workouts with the chest press machine

  • Chest: Machines 3 × 10–12, rest 2 min
