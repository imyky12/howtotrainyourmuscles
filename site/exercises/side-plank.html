How to do the side plank

The side plank is a bodyweight exercise for the obliques. It mainly works the obliques and side glute. Half a plank, twice the wobble.

Muscles worked

  • Obliquesmain
  • Side glutemain

Step by step

  1. Elbow under the shoulder forearm forward

    Elbow under the shoulder, forearm pointing forward, feet stacked.

  2. Lift the hips body straight

    Lift the hips until the body is one straight line.

  3. Hips stacked don’t roll

    Keep the hips stacked and the chest facing sideways.

  4. Hold and breathe then switch sides

    Breathe and hold, then switch sides. Keep both sides within about 5 seconds of each other.

3 common side plank mistakes

1Hips sag

The hips drop about 12 cm toward the floor, like a banana.

Right away
The obliques and side glute stop working.
Over time
The drill does nothing.
The fix
Hips up, long straight line.

2Rolling forward

The chest turns toward the floor and the pelvis rolls 25–30°.

Right away
The load leaves the obliques for the shoulder.
Over time
A sore shoulder.
The fix
Chest faces the wall, not the floor.

3Shoulder shrugged

The elbow sits 10 cm in front of the shoulder and the shoulder rides up toward the ear.

Right away
The shoulder joint is stressed.
Over time
A sore front shoulder.
The fix
Push the floor away, elbow under.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 holds of 15–20 s per side, from the knees if needed.

Workouts with the side plank

  • Core: All three jobs 2 × 20–40 s each side, rest 45 s
  • Core: Mat only, back friendly 2 × 20–30 s each side, rest 45 s
