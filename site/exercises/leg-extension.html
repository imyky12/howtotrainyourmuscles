How to do the leg extension

The leg extension is a machine exercise for the front of the thigh. It mainly works the quads (rectus femoris). Kick it straight. Hold. Feel the quad glow.

Muscles worked

  • Quads (rectus femoris)main

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 2 sec

    Lower the pad slowly, about 2 seconds.

  2. Knees at 90° pad on the shins

    Knees back to about 90°. The back pad is reclined so the front of the thigh is stretched.

  3. Kick straight one smooth push

    Straighten the legs in one smooth push. Hips stay on the seat.

  4. Squeeze one second

    Legs straight. Squeeze the front of the thigh for a second.

3 common leg extension mistakes

1Hips lift

The hips rise off the seat and the torso rocks back to swing the weight up.

Right away
Momentum, not the quads, lifts it.
Over time
No progress, and bruises from the pad.
The fix
Hips down, grip the handles.

2Dropping the weight

The pad drops in under half a second and the plates clank.

Right away
Half the rep, the lowering, is wasted.
Over time
Slower growth.
The fix
Lower for two seconds.

3Half reps

The legs stop at about 130° and never straighten.

Right away
The top squeeze, where the quads peak, is missing.
Over time
Less growth of the front of the thigh.
The fix
All the way straight.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–15: the best first quad exercise. If the kneecap hurts at the top, stop short of straight.

Workouts with the leg extension

  • Legs: Full legs 2 × 10–15, rest 90 s
  • Legs: Glutes and machines 2 × 12–15, rest 90 s
