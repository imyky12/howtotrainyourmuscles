How to do the pull-up

The pull-up is a bodyweight exercise for the lats. It mainly works the lats, with help from the biceps and lower traps. Chin over the bar. Gravity has opinions; ignore them.

Step by step

  1. Lower slowly 2 sec

    Lower yourself slowly, about 2 seconds, keeping the legs still.

  2. Full stretch arms straight

    Hang with straight arms. Let the shoulder blades rise: this is the lats’ stretch.

  3. Pull chin over bar elbows down

    Pull the shoulders down first, then drive the elbows down until your chin passes the bar.

  4. Squeeze chin above the bar

    Chin over the bar, chest close to it. Pause for a moment.

3 common pull-up mistakes

1Kipping

The hips swing forward and back, so the legs throw you up to the bar.

Right away
Momentum does the work, so the lats get less of it.
Over time
You plateau, because the muscle never gets the full load.
The fix
Legs still. Pull with your back.

2Half reps

The chin stops 5–10 cm below the bar at the top.

Right away
The top half of the pull, where the lats and lower traps squeeze, is skipped.
Over time
Slower strength gains at the top of the movement.
The fix
Chin over the bar, every rep.

3No full hang

The elbows stay bent at about 130° at the bottom.

Right away
You cut out the stretched range, the part that grows muscle most.
Over time
Less growth from the same number of reps.
The fix
Straight arms at the bottom.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of as many clean reps as you can, minus two. Can’t do one yet? Jump up and lower yourself in 3–5 seconds.

Workouts with the pull-up

  • Back: Full back 2 × as many clean reps as you can, minus 2, rest 2 min
