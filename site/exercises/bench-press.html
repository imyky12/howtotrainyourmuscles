How to do the bench press

The bench press is a barbell exercise for the whole chest. It mainly works the chest, with help from the front shoulder and triceps.

Muscles worked

  • Chestmain
  • Front shoulderhelping
  • Tricepshelping

Step by step

  1. Bring it down 2 sec, breathe in

    Lower the bar slowly, about 2 seconds, to the lower part of your chest. Breathe in on the way down. Elbows about 45° from your body.

  2. Light touch on the chest

    Let the bar just touch your chest, around nipple level. No bounce, so you stay in control. Your chest works hardest right here.

  3. Push it up breathe out

    Push the bar up and slightly back toward your face. Breathe out as you push, and press your feet into the floor.

  4. Arms straight over the shoulders

    Finish with straight arms and the bar over your shoulders. Your triceps finish the job. Breathe, then start the next rep.

Setup checklist

  • Eyes under the bar, feet flat, shoulder blades pinched.
  • Grip about 1.5× shoulder width, wrists stacked over elbows.
  • Lower in about 2 seconds to the lower chest, elbows 45–60° from your body.
  • Light touch, no bounce. Press up and slightly back over the shoulders.

4 common bench press mistakes

1Elbows straight out (90°)

Arms make a T. The bar comes down high on the chest.

Right away
The front of your shoulders takes the weight; your chest works less.
Over time
Often leads to pain at the front of the shoulder. With heavy weight, a higher risk of a shoulder strain or a torn chest muscle.
The fix
Elbows about 45° from your body, like an arrow. Bar to the lower chest.

2Bar drops and bounces

The bar drops fast and springs off the ribs.

Right away
The bounce does the hardest part, so your chest gets less work. You lose control of the bar.
Over time
Bruised breastbone or ribs, and less muscle growth for the same effort.
The fix
About 2 seconds down, a light touch, then push.

3Hips lift off the bench

Your bottom lifts off the bench as you push.

Right away
Your lower back bends and takes the strain. The lift feels easier than it really is.
Over time
Lower-back pain. You overestimate your strength, and in competitions the lift doesn’t count.
The fix
Keep your bottom on the bench. Push your feet into the floor.

4Bar comes down too high

The bar comes down to the upper chest or neck.

Right away
Your elbows flare and the shoulders take the strain.
Over time
Shoulder pain. If your grip slips, the bar is over your neck and face.
The fix
Aim for the lower chest, around nipple level, forearms straight up and down.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 8–12; start with the empty bar (20 kg).

Workouts with the bench press

  • Chest: Classic barbell 3 × 6–10, rest 2–3 min
