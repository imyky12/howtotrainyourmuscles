How to do the overhead press

The overhead press is a barbell exercise for the front shoulders. It mainly works the front shoulders and side shoulders, with help from the triceps. Press straight up. Your lower back is not a hinge.

Step by step

  1. Lower to the upper chest 2 sec

    Lower the bar along the same straight line to the top of your chest.

  2. Bar on the chest elbows under

    Bar on the upper chest, elbows slightly in front, wrists stacked.

  3. Press straight up head back, then through

    Press straight up. Tip the head back so the bar clears the face.

  4. Lock out bar over the feet

    Head through, arms straight, bar over the middle of the foot. A small shrug at the top is correct.

3 common overhead press mistakes

1Leaning back

The torso leans back 20–30° with the ribs flared as you press.

Right away
It turns into an incline press and the lower back takes the load.
Over time
A sore lower back after press days.
The fix
Squeeze glutes, ribs down.

2Bar drifts forward

The bar travels 10–15 cm in front of the feet and finishes in front of the shoulders.

Right away
The shoulder works at a longer lever, so less weight goes up.
Over time
Elbow and shoulder strain from the forward lean.
The fix
Head through, bar over the ears.

3Wrists bent back

The elbows sit behind the bar at the start and the wrists bend back about 40°.

Right away
The press starts from a weak position.
Over time
Wrist pain.
The fix
Elbows under, knuckles up.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 6–10 with the empty 20 kg bar or a light technique bar.
