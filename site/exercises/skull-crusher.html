How to do the skull crusher

The skull crusher is an ez bar exercise for the triceps (long head). It mainly works the triceps. Named for the mistake. Aim behind the head.

Muscles worked

  • Tricepsmain

Step by step

  1. Bend the elbows 3 sec, slowly

    Bend the elbows slowly, about 3 seconds. The upper arms stay angled slightly back toward the head.

  2. Bar behind the head elbows up

    The bar passes the hairline and finishes behind the head, not on it.

  3. Extend upper arms still

    Straighten the arms without moving the upper arms.

  4. Lock out arms angled back

    Lock out with the arms angled slightly back, so the triceps stay loaded.

3 common skull crusher mistakes

1Elbows flare out

The elbows spread from about 35 cm to 60 cm apart.

Right away
The shoulders and chest join in and the elbows take a sideways load.
Over time
Elbow-tendon pain: the classic skull-crusher complaint.
The fix
Elbows in, pointing up.

2Upper arms rock

The upper arms swing back and forth by about 40° every rep.

Right away
The lats and shoulders move the bar.
Over time
Triceps undertrained.
The fix
Freeze the upper arms.

3Bar to the forehead

The bar stops just above the forehead with the elbows bent only about 70°.

Right away
You skip the stretched range.
Over time
Less growth, and a real risk of hitting your forehead.
The fix
Past the head, not onto it.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12 with the empty EZ bar (about 10 kg). If your elbows hurt, use the overhead rope instead.

Workouts with the skull crusher

  • Triceps: Free weights only 3 × 10–12, rest 90 s
