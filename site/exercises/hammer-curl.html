How to do the hammer curl

The hammer curl is a dumbbell exercise for the forearms & brachialis. It mainly works the forearms (brachioradialis) and biceps. Thumbs up. Your forearms finally get invited.

Muscles worked

  • Forearms (brachioradialis)main
  • Bicepsmain

Step by step

  1. Lower under control 2 sec

    Lower the dumbbells under control.

  2. Arms hang thumbs forward

    Arms hang at the sides, thumbs pointing forward.

  3. Curl to the shoulder thumbs up

    Curl up with the thumbs up, finishing just outside the front of the shoulders.

  4. Squeeze briefly

    Squeeze briefly. The thumbs-up grip lets most people lift a bit more and is easy on the wrists.

3 common hammer curl mistakes

1Swinging

The torso leans back 15° or more and the knees bounce.

Right away
Momentum lifts the weight.
Over time
Lower-back strain and no arm growth.
The fix
Only the forearm moves.

2Cross-body drift

The hands drift 10 cm or more inside the shoulders at the top.

Right away
Load shifts to the front shoulders when it’s not on purpose.
Over time
Uneven arms.
The fix
Straight up, outside the shoulder.

3Wrist curling

The wrists bend 20–30° at the top of each rep.

Right away
The forearm flexors steal the finish.
Over time
Wrist-tendon irritation.
The fix
Wrist straight, like a hammer handle.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–12, 1–2 kg heavier than your dumbbell curl. Easiest curl on the wrists.

Workouts with the hammer curl

  • Biceps: Peak and stretch 2 × 12, rest 60 s
  • Biceps: Cables and isolation 2 × 12–15, rest 60 s
