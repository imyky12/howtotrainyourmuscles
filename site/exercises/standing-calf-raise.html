How to do the standing calf raise

The standing calf raise is a machine exercise for the calves. It mainly works the calves (gastrocnemius) and soleus. Tiptoes, but make it heavy.

Muscles worked

  • Calves (gastrocnemius)main
  • Soleusmain

Step by step

  1. Lower the heels 2 sec

    Lower the heels slowly below the step.

  2. Pause in the stretch one second

    Pause for a second with the calves fully stretched: this part grows them most.

  3. Rise onto the toes high

    Rise as high as you can onto the balls of your feet. Legs stay straight.

  4. Hold one second

    Hold at the top for a second, then lower.

3 common standing calf raise mistakes

1Bouncing

The heels bounce off the bottom with no pause, under a second per rep.

Right away
The Achilles tendon springs the weight; the muscle works less.
Over time
Calves don’t grow; Achilles irritation.
The fix
Pause at the bottom, no bounce.

2Half range

The heels never go below the step.

Right away
It skips the stretched position.
Over time
About half the growth.
The fix
Heels below the step.

3Knees bend

The knees bend on each rep to help push.

Right away
The gastrocnemius goes slack.
Over time
It quietly becomes a soleus-only exercise.
The fix
Legs straight, calves only.

Sets and reps for beginners

3 sets of 10–15 with a slow stretch.

Workouts with the standing calf raise

  • Legs: Full legs 3 × 10–15, 1 s stretch, rest 90 s
  • Legs: Glutes and machines 1 × 10–15, rest 90 s
