Biceps workout plans

Peak and stretch Any level · about 35 min

The barbell curl is the heaviest, so it goes first. The incline curl stretches the long head, the preacher curl works the short head, and the hammer curl brings in the forearms.

Barbell curl3 × 8–1090 s

Cables and isolation Any level · about 30 min

The cable keeps tension at the top where a bar goes light. The concentration curl scored the highest biceps activity in the ACE study with the least shoulder help.
