Back workout plans

Full back Intermediate · about 70 min

The deadlift goes first while you’re fresh: the heaviest load for the lower back. Pull-ups and rows cover the lats and the middle back, the straight-arm pulldown isolates the lats, shrugs finish the upper traps. Can’t do 5 pull-ups yet? Swap in the lat pulldown.
