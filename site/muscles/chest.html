Chest workout plans

Classic barbell Intermediate · about 60 min

Heavy presses first while you’re fresh: incline for the upper chest, flat for the middle. Dips and high-to-low cables finish the lower chest.

ExerciseSets × repsRest
Incline bench press3 × 6–102–3 min
Bench press3 × 6–102–3 min
Chest dip2 × 8–122 min
Cable crossover, high to low2 × 12–151–1.5 min

Dumbbells Beginner to intermediate · about 50 min

Dumbbells let your hands go deeper, so every press stretches the chest more. The fly trains the stretch; dips cover the lower chest.

ExerciseSets × repsRest
Incline dumbbell press3 × 8–122 min
Dumbbell bench press3 × 8–122 min
Dumbbell fly2 × 12–151–1.5 min
Chest dip2 × 6–102 min

Machines Beginner, first months · about 45 min

Machines guide the path, so you can learn the movement and safely train hard. Low-to-high cables hit the upper chest, high-to-low the lower chest.

ExerciseSets × repsRest
Chest press machine3 × 10–122 min
Cable crossover, low to high2 × 12–151–1.5 min
Pec deck3 × 12–151–1.5 min
Cable crossover, high to low2 × 12–151–1.5 min

No equipment Any level · about 25 min

Push-ups train the whole chest; dips add the lower chest. Make push-ups harder by slowing the lowering to 3 seconds.

ExerciseSets × repsRest
Push-up3 × as many clean reps as you can, minus 21.5 min
