Triceps workout plans

Heavy press, then the long head Intermediate · about 40 min

The close-grip bench is the heaviest triceps lift, so it goes first. The overhead extension stretches the long head (it grew it most in Maeo 2023), the pushdown finishes the outer heads, dips add bodyweight work.
