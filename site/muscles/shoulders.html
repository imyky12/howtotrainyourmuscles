Shoulders workout plans

Barbell and raises Intermediate · about 50 min

The overhead press moves the most weight, so it comes first. Then one raise for each head: side, rear, and the face pull for the rear shoulders and rotator cuff. The front raise is optional: presses already work the front hardest.
