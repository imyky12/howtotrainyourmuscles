Legs workout plans

Full legs Intermediate · about 60 min

The squat goes first while you’re fresh. The Romanian deadlift adds the hinge for the hamstrings, the split squat trains one leg at a time, the leg extension hits the part of the quads the squat skips, and calves finish.

Glutes and machines Any level · about 60 min

Use it as your second leg day of the week: the hip thrust takes the squat’s place for the glutes, the leg press for the quads, and both calf machines cover the gastrocnemius and the soleus.
